Rui: Voice Assistant

The next thing I am building is a voice assistant. There are people who may have difficulty using their hands, and there are also people like me who work across three screens and sometimes have their hands occupied. Imagine washing your face, opening your laptop, and wanting to open Chrome, VS Code, or another application. You have to use the mouse, click on the application, and wait for it to open while still trying to rub your face. It is not ideal. So, I decided to make a voice assistant.

It works both offline and online, although I am still developing the online part. When you are not connected to the internet, you can ask it to open certain applications, tell you a joke, or tell you what day it is. These functions do not require an internet connection, so you can simply say, "Open this" or "Open that," and it does it.

I called it RUI, which is basically my Chinese name. When connected to the internet, I want it to be able to do more, such as opening Gmail and typing a message to someone. I want to make it interactive so that it can tell jokes, provide weather information, or read emails, because sometimes I really want a personal assistant at this point. What I noticed is that many agents available online are not very good. They might use an AI model that consumes a lot of tokens and resources without accomplishing much. I want to build something that is genuinely useful and efficient.

→ Rui
